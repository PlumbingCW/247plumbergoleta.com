Why each town has its own page

Water and sewer service changes from one town to the next. Goleta Water District serves much of the Goleta Valley, while Santa Barbara and Montecito have their own water providers, and sanitary districts handle sewage in different places. That affects which agency you call, what permit applies and what kind of pipe you are likely to find.

Housing changes too. Rental buildings near the university, hillside homes in the foothills and large coastal properties each have different plumbing problems. The town pages explain what we see most often and what to do before the plumber arrives.

What dispatch can and cannot promise

Dispatch is 24/7 in every town on this page. Travel time depends on traffic, the time of day and where the plumber is, so the dispatcher gives you the next step instead of a fixed arrival window.

Some properties, such as homes on private roads or gated estates, need access details. Tell the dispatcher about gates and codes on the first call. For a first look at the work involved, browse the plumbing services.
