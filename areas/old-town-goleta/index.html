Where Old Town Goleta sits

Old Town is part of Goleta, the city where our shop sits on Hollister Avenue. It grew from two farm-era towns. The City of Goleta's 2022 historic resources inventory says La Patera was established in 1869 at Fairview and Hollister avenues, and La Goleta in 1869 on the north side of Hollister at Patterson Avenue.

The same inventory describes an original residential neighborhood that grew next to the commercial corridor between the two towns. It lists surviving houses on Aguila Avenue, Chapel Street and Nectarine Avenue dated to about 1915 through 1920.

That tells you some Old Town houses are a century old. It does not tell you what is inside any one wall. Pipe material, repiping history and the year of a water heater come from county records, the seller's paperwork and a look on site, not from the neighborhood name.

The city does not publish exact boundaries for Old Town in the sources we checked, so tell the dispatcher your street and the nearest cross street.

What the street layout means for a plumbing visit

These are practical notes drawn from reporting on Old Town streets, not claims about any one address.

Narrow side streets

Residents quoted by the Santa Barbara Independent in 2018 described parking as already difficult and the streets as narrow. Tell the dispatcher where a van can stop and whether the driveway is shared.

Parking on Hollister Avenue

After the 2024 restriping, the Independent counted 53 diagonal back-in spaces on Hollister. Pulling out takes care, so a visit to a shop front may start at a side-street space.

Construction and traffic

The same report links slow traffic partly to Project Connect, which includes rebuilding the San Jose Creek bridge near State Route 217. Give a cross street so the plumber chooses a clear approach.

Homes beside businesses

Because homes and shops share the corridor, one call may be a kitchen drain at a house and the next a restaurant grease line. Commercial plumbing is handled through the same line.

Plumbing jobs we take in Old Town

Most calls fall into a handful of groups. Each service page explains the signs, what to do first and what the repair involves.

Hard water and what it does inside Goleta water heaters

Goleta Water District's 2025 water quality report gives a customer average hardness of 21 grains per gallon, and the district's own page calls the water very hard. That applies across the district, Old Town included. Neighbors in Ellwood see the same water.

Hard water leaves scale on heating elements and settles as sediment in a tank. A noisy heater or a drop in hot water volume is a common sign. A tank flush can help, and a softener or filter is an option if you want softer water.

Street flooding and plumbing are different problems

At a 2018 neighborhood meeting, a resident raised standing water on Magnolia Street. The Independent reported that drainage would be handled in a separate project from the sidewalk work.

Water standing in the street is a storm drain matter for the city. Water coming up inside your building through a floor drain, tub or cleanout is a sewer or drain problem, and that is a plumbing call. If it happens during heavy rain, shut off water use in the building and call.
