What to tell the dispatcher

Say what is happening and where the water or sewage is. Give the full address and a callback number, and say whether the main valve is already off.

Not sure what service you need? See the plumbing services list or check whether your town is on the service areas page. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
