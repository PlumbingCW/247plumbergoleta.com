Emergency plumbing

Plumbing problems we answer around the clock

These are the problems that cannot wait for Monday. Each page explains the warning signs, what to do first and what the repair involves. The full list is on the plumbing services page.

Drain Cleaning

Slow sinks, blocked toilets and main-line backups cleared with snakes, cables and jetting, day or night.

Water Heater Repair

Leaks, no hot water, pilot and igniter faults, bad elements and relief valves fixed on electric, gas and hybrid heaters.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed, leaking or flooded tanks removed and replaced with gas, electric or heat pump units, strapped and connected to code.

Leak Detection

Hidden leaks found with acoustic listening, thermal imaging, pressure tests and cameras, before walls and floors are opened.

Sewer Line Repair

Spot repairs and full lateral replacement for broken, rooted or collapsed sewer lines, with a camera and clear price first.

Burst Pipe Repair

Burst, cracked and leaking pipes in copper, PEX, CPVC, galvanized and drain lines stopped and repaired day or night.

Slab Leak Repair

Leaks in lines under a concrete floor found without guesswork, then fixed by spot repair, rerouting or repiping.

Toilet Repair

Overflowing, leaking and rocking toilets repaired or replaced, including wax rings, flanges and tank parts.

Faucet and Sink Repair

Dripping faucets, broken stop valves, shower and tub leaks and spigot repairs, with a clear price before work starts.

Commercial Plumbing

After-hours plumbing for restaurants, offices and retail, from floor drains and grease traps to backflow and storm lines.

Planned work is covered too, including water heater installation, repiping and sewer camera inspection.

Why call us

What you get when you call

A plumbing emergency is stressful enough. The way the call is handled should lower the pressure, not add to it.

1

A person answers day or night

The 24/7 line is staffed, so a pipe that fails at 3 a.m. gets the same dispatch as a weekday call.

2

A clear price before work starts

The plumber explains the problem and the price first. Work begins after you say yes.

3

Local water knowledge

Goleta Water District reports a customer average of 21 grains per gallon, which is very hard water. Scale in heaters, valves and fixtures is a daily repair topic here.

4

Licensed and insured

Work is done by licensed plumbers. Repairs follow the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and you can look up any California contractor on the Contractors State License Board website.
