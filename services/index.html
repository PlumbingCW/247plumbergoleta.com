How to choose the right page

Start with what you can see or hear. Water on the floor, a meter that keeps spinning or a warm patch underfoot points toward leak detection or slab leak repair. A drain that gurgles or backs up into a second fixture points toward sewer line cleaning.

If the water heater is the problem, the choice is usually repair or replacement. A plumber can tell you which makes sense after checking the tank age, the leak location and the hard water scale inside. Start with water heater repair and move to replacement if the tank itself has failed.

Homes and businesses in the Goleta Valley share one useful fact. Goleta Water District reports an average of 21 grains per gallon, so scale builds up in heaters, valves and fixtures faster than many owners expect. Water softener and filtration work is one way to slow that down.

Emergency or planned?

An emergency is anything where water, sewage or gas is already causing damage or could soon. Planned work is a job you choose to do, such as an upgrade, an inspection or a new fixture.

When in doubt, call. The dispatcher will tell you honestly whether the visit should happen tonight or can wait for a scheduled slot. Business owners can read about commercial plumbing after-hours service, and homeowners planning a remodel can start with plumbing installation.
